# research

## Purpose

- Source material gathered for the workflow audit and the evaluations derived from it
- Every file answers two separate questions: what the source says, and what we conclude for this project

## Ownership

- `videos/` — one note per video plus syntheses of video triage runs
- `prompts/` — prompts handed to external models (Gemini, NotebookLM); each prompt names its output file and, once run, the date and where the synthesis lives
- `reports/` — raw outputs returned by external models, unedited; syntheses and assessments go to `videos/` or a sibling topic folder, never into `reports/`
- `workflow/` — notes and syntheses on agentic workflow research (Deep Research runs, surveys); Obsidian-flavoured markdown with mermaid diagrams is allowed here
- File names follow `strategy/naming.md`: `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-<kind>[-<variant>].md`, prompt and its report share the topic

## Local Contracts

- Note header lists: URL, channel, duration, publish date, processing method (transcript, Gemini, watched by human)
- Timestamps use `mm:ss` (or `h:mm:ss` above one hour) and point at the moment a claim starts
- Keep "Treść" (what the source says) and "Ocena" (our assessment) in separate sections; never mix them
- Sponsored segments and product upsells are marked, not summarized
- Claims about tools that were not verified against the user's real setup are marked `[do weryfikacji]`

## Work Guidance

- Notes are in Polish; quotes from sources stay in the original language
- Prompts for external models state the user's goals from `README.md` explicitly so relevance is judged against them, not in general
